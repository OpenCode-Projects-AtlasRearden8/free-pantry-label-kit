Buttermilk Pancake Mix

weekend shortcut
just add egg, milk, butter

In the mix (store in a big jar)

  • 3 cups flour
  • 3 Tbsp sugar + 4 tsp baking powder + 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
To cook: 2 cups mix + 2 eggs + 2 cups buttermilk + ¼ cup melted butter. Use dry mix within 3 months.

Chocolate Chip Cookie Mix

one bowl, one dough
chocolate chips ride on top, not in the jar

In the mix

  • 3½ cups flour + 1 tsp baking soda + 1½ tsp salt
  • 1¼ cups brown sugar, packed (shake-free: stir)
To bake: 1 cup mix + ½ cup softened butter + 1 egg + splash vanilla, then fold in 1 cup chips. Use dry mix within 3 months.

Brownie Mix

fudgy by default
bake more often

In the mix

  • 2 cups sugar + 1 cup flour
  • ⅔ cup cocoa powder
  • ½ cup chopped chocolate or chips (add at bake time)
  • ½ tsp salt + ½ tsp baking powder
To bake: mix + 4 eggs + 1 cup melted butter + 1 tsp vanilla; 8×8 pan, 28–32 min at 350°F. Use within 3 months.

Cornbread Mix

slightly sweet
pairs with the Saturday chili card

In the mix

  • 1 cup each: flour, cornmeal, sugar
  • 1 Tbsp baking powder + 1 tsp salt
To bake: mix + 2 eggs + 1 cup milk + ⅓ cup oil; 8×8 pan, 20–25 min at 400°F. Use within 3 months.

Banana Muffin Mix

rescues brown bananas
freeze extras bananas whole; thaw to bake

In the mix

  • 2 cups flour + ¾ cup sugar
  • 1 Tbsp baking powder + ½ tsp baking soda + ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
To bake: mix + 3 ripe mashed bananas + 1 egg + ⅓ cup oil; 12 muffins, 18–22 min at 375°F. Use within 3 months.

Dinner Roll Mix

Sunday bread
yeast lives in the fridge, not in the mix

In the mix

  • 4 cups bread flour + ¼ cup sugar + 1½ tsp salt
To bake: mix + 1 cup warm milk + 2¼ tsp yeast + 2 eggs + ½ cup soft butter; knead, rise 1 hr, shape 12 rolls, rise 40 min, bake 18–20 min at 375°F. Use within 3 months.
