How to use it: write the "date out" on every freezer label as the date in here plus this window — the math happens once, while the food goes in. When in doubt: look, smell, then toss. Freezer-burned food is safe; smelly fridge food is not. This chart is friendly education, not a safety certification; your local extension office and the date printed on packaging always win.

Weekly

Scan the fridge shelf on trash night; anything 3+ days old becomes lunch or goes to the freezer.

Monthly

Freezer amnesty day: pull everything forward, re-date anything missing a "date out" label.

Seasonally

Check spice jars against this chart; send faded ones to "soup builder" duty — strong smell only matters when it has faded.
