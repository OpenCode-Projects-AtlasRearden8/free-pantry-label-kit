Taco Blend

per 1 lb meat
tame the heat by halving the chipotle

Mix together, shake, store

  • 4 tsp chili powder
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp each: garlic powder, paprika, oregano
  • ½ tsp each: onion powder, salt, crushed chipotle
Use within: 6 months · Brown 1 lb meat + blend + ⅓ cup water

Curry Powder

mild & warm
toast lightly in a dry pan for a deeper flavor

Mix together, shake, store

  • 2 Tbsp each: coriander, cumin, turmeric
  • 1 tsp each: ginger, mustard seed
  • ½ tsp each: black pepper, cayenne (optional), cinnamon
Use within: 6 months · Start with 1 Tbsp per pot of stew

Pumpkin Pie Spice

fall in a jar
swap in gingerbread by adding 1 tsp more ginger

Mix together, shake, store

  • 3 Tbsp cinnamon
  • 2 tsp each: ginger, nutmeg
  • 1 tsp each: allspice, cloves
Use within: 6 months · 1–2 tsp per pie, latte, or quick bread

Ranch Seasoning

dip, dress, repeat
sprinkle on popcorn immediately

Mix together, shake, store

  • 2 Tbsp each: dried parsley, dried dill
  • 1 Tbsp each: garlic powder, onion powder, dried chives
  • 1 tsp each: salt, black pepper
Use within: 3 months · Stir 1 Tbsp into 1 cup sour cream or yogurt

Italian Herb Blend

garden shaker
finish coarser for rubs, finer for dressings

Mix together, shake, store

  • 2 Tbsp each: dried basil, dried oregano
  • 1 Tbsp each: dried rosemary, dried thyme
  • 1 tsp each: garlic powder, red pepper flakes
Use within: 6 months · Sprinkle on roasts, sauces, and focaccia

Saturday Chili Powder

big pot energy
named for the day a huge pot gets made

Mix together, shake, store

  • 3 Tbsp each: ancho chili powder, cumin
  • 1 Tbsp each: paprika, oregano, cocoa powder
  • 2 tsp each: garlic powder, cornmeal (thickener)
Use within: 6 months · 2–3 Tbsp per pot of chili or beans
